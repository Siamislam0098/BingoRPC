# BingoRPC privacy policy

BingoRPC shows what you watch on streaming sites as your Discord status.

**What it reads (only on sites you added yourself)**
- The video's current time, length, and play/pause state
- The page title, the site name, and the poster image address

**Where it goes**
- Only to the BingoRPC Helper running on your own computer, and from there to the Discord desktop app on your computer, which shows it as your status (so the people who can see your Discord status can see it).
- To show the poster, the helper requests the poster image address from your computer. The site's image server can see that request.
- The site icon in your status is loaded from Google's favicon service (`google.com/s2/favicons`) using the site's domain.

**What it does not do**
- No account, no analytics, no tracking, no ads.
- Nothing is sent to the BingoRPC developer.
- Nothing is stored except your list of added sites and on/off choices, kept in your browser.
- It does not read any site you did not add.

**Removing it**
Remove a site in the extension popup (this takes back the permission), uninstall the extension in your browser, and uninstall "BingoRPC Helper" in Windows Settings > Apps.

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
